There is a moment between considering something and doing it where most people wait. They wait for permission. A sign. An invitation. Someone to tell them they are ready.

That permission does not come. Not because you do not deserve it. Because the system is not designed to give it. The system is designed to filter. To sort. To select. Not to invite.

The women who move forward are not the ones who received permission. They are the ones who realized permission was never required.

This is not rebellion. It is observation. The door was never locked. You were standing in front of it, waiting for someone to open it, while people walked through on either side.

Starting something new — a skill, a career direction, a way of thinking — does not require qualifications. It requires a decision. One decision, followed by one action. That is it.

AI is full of doors like this. The tools are free. The learning resources are abundant. The only barrier is the belief that you need someone to tell you to begin.

You do not.

If you have been waiting for a sign, this is it. Not because this post has any authority. Because it is the only sign you are going to get.

Start.

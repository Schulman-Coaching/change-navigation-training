# First Customer Sprint — 14 Days

The goal is five free 20-minute Calendly calls booked. Not memberships. Not workshops. Five conversations with women who match a cohort.

That is the number that proves the funnel works. Bookings come before workshops. Workshops come before memberships. Skip a stage and the math breaks.

## What we are not doing

Not building more content. Not adding offers. Not redesigning sites.

The four sites carry 29 blog posts between them. The nurture sequence is written. The lead magnet exists. Stripe links work. The bottleneck is not the asset library. It is that nobody has been told CNT exists.

## Three unblocks before the sprint runs (Days 1–3)

These happen before outreach goes live. Without them, traffic lands on a half-built funnel.

**1. Point DNS at the four Vercel projects.**
Custom domains are configured but not resolving. Every link still points to `*.vercel.app`. That reads as unfinished. Fix the DNS, the sites become trustworthy.

**2. Stand up Resend for the contact form and nurture sequence.**
The 5-email sequence is written. It cannot fire without an ESP wired to `/api/contact`. Resend is the lightest path — sign up, drop the API key into the four Vercel projects, send the test message.

**3. Host the lead magnet PDF.**
The lead magnet is specced but not yet a file anywhere. Put it in Google Drive with a clean share link, or in `/public` on each site. Add the link as the reward at the end of the nurture.

Until these three things are true, do not start outreach. Sending strangers to a broken funnel is worse than waiting two days.

## The 14-day outreach rhythm (Days 4–17)

Daily volume matters more than polish. The pattern repeats every weekday.

**Each morning (30 minutes):**
- Five personal WhatsApp messages to women in your network who match a cohort. Not a pitch. A question: *"I am building something for women navigating [her situation]. Would you tell me what is hardest about it?"* That conversation is the free call.
- One LinkedIn DM to a frum professional woman, 35–50, in your second-degree network. Same question.

**Each afternoon (30 minutes):**
- One IG post on the cohort-matched account. Rotate Start, Build, Lead, Renew across the week. Use the creative in `IG_ADS.md`.
- One share of an existing blog post to a relevant WhatsApp group. One sentence of why this matters this week.

**Each Thursday:**
- One cold outreach to a local Jewish women's organization. Chabad sisterhoods, Emunah, Amit, NCJW, JWRP alumni groups, Hadassah, shul women's committees. One sentence: *"Would you let me run a free 20-minute preview workshop for your group?"*

**Each motzei Shabbos:**
- Count the week. Messages sent, replies received, calls booked. Adjust the angle, not the pace.

## What success looks like at Day 14

| Metric | Target | If you miss it |
|---|---|---|
| Messages sent (WA + LinkedIn + IG + outreach) | 100+ | Pace is too slow. Double daily volume. |
| Replies received | 25+ | Opener is too generic. Make it specific to a moment in her week. |
| Calls booked on Calendly | 5+ | Replies happen but do not convert. Add a soft CTA at the end of every thread. |
| Workshop signups (Stripe) | 1+ | Free call to workshop conversion is broken. Fix the call script. |

The five calls is the only number that matters. Everything else is a leading indicator.

## What blocks this from working

**No follow-through on personal messages.** Send five WA messages, three reply, those replies need a response within four hours. The window closes by evening.

**Treating outreach as broadcasting.** Mass DMs read as mass DMs. Each message is one sentence written for one person.

**Generic openers.** *"Hey, would love to chat about my new thing."* Dead on arrival. The opener is a question about her life. The conversation surfaces the need.

**Waiting for content marketing to work.** It will, in 6–18 months. The sprint is not that strategy. It is what produces revenue before the SEO compounds.

That is the loop. Five calls is week one's target. Adjust from there.

## Risks worth naming

- **Network fatigue.** The first 50 WA messages go to people who already know you. The next 50 are harder. By Day 10 the warm network is exhausted and the cold pipeline has to start producing. Build the org outreach list early.
- **Shabbos pacing.** The week loses a full day. Compress to four work days, not five. Front-load Sunday through Thursday.
- **Voice drift in DMs.** Personal messages tempt the voice toward casual hype. Keep the CNT voice even in WhatsApp. Direct, unhurried, no exclamation points.
